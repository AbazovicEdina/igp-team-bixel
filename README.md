## 🌊 The Idea

You are alone.
Deep below. In the dark.
Just you, your soundboard – and whatever it is that answers out there.

**Deep Signal** is an atmospheric horror game about curiosity, risk, and the silence between two tones.

You play an underwater researcher at a remote deep-sea station. Your only connection to the outside world: a physical soundboard made of buttons, wires, and raw metal. You send signals into the depths. You wait. You log what you hear.

Eventually, something answers.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay – How It Works

```
[ SEND SIGNAL ]  →  [ WAIT ]  →  [ LISTEN TO THE RESPONSE ]  →  [ LOG IT ]
        ↑                                                           |
        └───────────────────── Tension rises ───────────────────────┘
```

- **Press** a button on the physical soundboard
- **Listen** to what comes back – or to the silence
- **Log** your observations
- **Decide** whether to keep going

Tension grows with every action. Sounds become more unsettling. The heartbeat gets louder. Hallucinations creep in. Eventually, you have to make a decision.

---

## 🔧 What Makes It Special: A Physical Soundboard

The heart of the game isn't a controller – it's a self-built **hardware soundboard** made of wood, foam, aluminum foil, and wires, sending inputs directly into Unity.

The soundboard is part of the horror experience.

---

## 🌐 Atmosphere

- A claustrophobic underwater station
- A dark, metallic environment
- Sound design as the primary game mechanic
- No jump scares – just slowly mounting unease
- Heartbeat, interference, distortion
